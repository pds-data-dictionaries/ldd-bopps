The Balloon Observation Platform for Planetary Science (BOPPS) mission was an Earth-based 
stratospheric balloon with a gondola housing the refurbished Stratospheric Teraherts 
Observatory (STO) Telescope and infrared camera. It flew on 26 September 2014 and 
obtained images of asteroid (1) Ceres and comets C/2012 K1 (PanSTARRS), C/2013 A1
(Siding Spring), and C/214 E2 (Jacques).
